# Technology logos

Logos identify technology actually used in the prototype and do not imply endorsement or prizes. Brand rights remain with their owners; the project's MIT license does not relicense these marks.

- Solana and Cardano: existing bundled vector icons used in the network selector and chain actions. The Cardano icon retains its light color and receives a dark background for legibility on the retail page.
- Chainlink: official blue symbol from https://chain.link/brand-assets, downloaded from the asset URL published there: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f6b7190899f41fb70882d08/6aa7fef7f56d1f1d953c589d_Chainlink-Symbol-Blue.svg . Preserve proportions and clear space.
- NOWNodes: vector asset referenced by https://nownodes.io: https://nownodes.io/_next/static/media/vertical-logo.826ff54c.svg .

Assets were reviewed/downloaded on 7 October 2026. They are bundled locally, so a judge's browser does not depend on a third-party asset request.
